## Status language

Use these labels consistently:

- **Planned:** intended, but not yet implemented.
- **In development:** being built or integrated.
- **Testing:** implemented but not yet validated sufficiently.
- **Available:** publicly accessible and checked.
- **Complete:** acceptance criteria have been met and verified.

Do not mark a feature as available or complete based only on a plan or a generated website page.
