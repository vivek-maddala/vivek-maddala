# Lumovia

**By ForgePath**  
**Tagline:** Learn more. Earn more.

## Overview

Lumovia is a learning-platform concept designed to make structured learning accessible to a broad audience. Its direction is to organize educational content into courses and lessons, with activities that help learners check their understanding and track completion.

## Learning experience

The planned experience brings together:

- Course discovery and course pages.
- Lessons supported by text, images, and video.
- Classes or guided learning sessions where available.
- Quizzes and knowledge checks.
- A way for learners to raise doubts and seek clarification.
- Certificates for eligible completed courses.

The availability of each feature depends on implementation and testing. This page describes the product direction rather than asserting that every feature is live.

## Intended audience

Lumovia is intended for learners across age groups and subject areas. Course design, accessibility, and content quality should be adapted to the needs of each audience.

## Product principles

- Organize content into clear learning paths.
- Make lesson objectives and expectations easy to understand.
- Use quizzes to reinforce learning rather than simply rank learners.
- Clearly communicate course requirements and certificate criteria.
- Keep learner information and account details protected.

## Development priorities

1. Establish a clear course catalogue and course structure.
2. Ensure lessons work consistently across supported devices.
3. Validate quiz scoring and course-completion tracking.
4. Define transparent certificate eligibility.
5. Test doubt-support workflows and response quality.
6. Collect learner feedback and improve course usability.
