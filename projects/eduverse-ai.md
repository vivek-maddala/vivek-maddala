# EduVerse AI

**By ForgePath**  
**Positioning:** AI student operating system  
**Website:** https://eduverseai.base44.app/

## Overview

EduVerse AI is an education-focused platform being developed to help students learn more effectively, understand academic concepts, identify learning gaps, and practise with purpose. The initial audience is school students in India, with an ambition to support additional curricula and regions over time.

## The problem

Students may have difficulty identifying which concepts they have not understood, finding explanations that match their syllabus, and turning mistakes into a useful study plan. A collection of disconnected study resources does not always provide a clear learning journey.

## Product direction

EduVerse AI is intended to bring several learning activities into one student experience:

- **AI learning support:** explain concepts and respond to study questions.
- **Curriculum-aware learning:** use relevant textbooks and learning material supplied or approved for the platform.
- **Self-assessment:** help students identify topics they understand and topics that need more practice.
- **Personalized practice:** recommend revision and practice based on assessment results.
- **Mock tests:** support practice by topic, chapter, exercise, or broader syllabus.
- **Progress insights:** summarize performance for a particular assessment and identify priority areas.
- **Educator and parent support:** provide appropriate progress views and tools where those features are implemented.

These bullets describe the intended product direction; they are not a guarantee that every capability is currently available.

## Intended learning journey

1. A student selects their class, subject, and topic.
2. The student studies an explanation or asks a question.
3. The student completes an assessment or practice activity.
4. The platform identifies areas that may need revision.
5. The student follows a focused practice plan.
6. The student reviews results and repeats the learning cycle.

## Audience and initial scope

- Initial focus: school students in India, especially Classes 6–10.
- Curriculum direction: support for relevant school curricula and student-provided learning materials.
- Longer-term direction: expand curriculum and geographic coverage based on validation and demand.

## Principles

- Make explanations understandable and age-appropriate.
- Treat assessment results as guidance, not labels of a student's ability.
- Keep student information private and provide appropriate access controls.
- Be transparent when AI output may be incomplete or require teacher verification.
- Test reliability before describing a feature as production-ready.

## Current status

EduVerse AI is an evolving product. The public website is the best place to check current availability. Some AI capabilities have previously required further integration and reliability work; this documentation therefore avoids presenting the full planned feature set as completed.
