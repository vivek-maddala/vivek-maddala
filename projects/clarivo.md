# Clarivo

**By ForgePath**  
**Focus:** Career exploration and guidance

## Overview

Clarivo is a career-guidance platform being developed to help students and working professionals explore possible education and career directions. It aims to make career exploration more structured by considering a person's interests, strengths, skills, goals, and learning experiences.

## The problem

Choosing a subject stream, course, or career direction can feel difficult when people have limited access to organized information or are unsure how their interests and strengths connect to possible pathways.

## Product direction

Clarivo is intended to support:

- Guided exploration of interests and skills.
- Information about education and career pathways.
- Reflection on strengths, goals, and areas to develop.
- Suggestions that help users investigate relevant options.
- Practical next steps, such as skills to build or questions to ask a qualified mentor.

Recommendations should be treated as starting points for exploration, not definitive decisions or guarantees of career outcomes.

## Connection with EduVerse AI

Clarivo and EduVerse AI are separate platforms with different purposes. Where a learner chooses to use both, relevant subject-strength insights from EduVerse AI may help inform career exploration in Clarivo. Clarivo can also introduce students to EduVerse AI when they want to explore academic strengths in more depth.

Any sharing of learner information should be explicit, limited to what is necessary, and controlled by the user and applicable guardian or school permissions.

## Intended audience

- Students exploring subjects, streams, courses, and future study options.
- Working professionals considering skill development or career changes.

## Product principles

- Encourage informed exploration instead of making decisions for users.
- Explain why a pathway may be relevant.
- Avoid guarantees about jobs, salaries, admissions, or outcomes.
- Respect privacy and obtain consent before using personal learning information.
- Encourage discussion with trusted adults, educators, and qualified career professionals for important decisions.

## Development priorities

1. Define a transparent career-exploration questionnaire and user journey.
2. Build a clear, explainable recommendation experience.
3. Validate pathway information and keep it current.
4. Establish consent-based connections with EduVerse AI.
5. Test accessibility, privacy, and recommendation quality.

## Current status

Clarivo is an evolving project. This document describes its intended purpose and development direction; it does not claim that every listed capability is currently implemented or available.
